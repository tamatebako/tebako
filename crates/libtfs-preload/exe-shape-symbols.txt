# The exe shape's exported surface (spec 22 §3.6) — the symbols the
# link-unit scoping must NOT rename, because renaming ends their
# preemption of the process's libc. One name per line, the ELF logical
# spelling (the Mach-O path prepends its underscore itself).
#
# Owner: sys/mod.rs is the definition; this file is the link-unit's copy
# of it, and `tests/exe_shape_surface.rs` proves the two agree. The exec
# class (dlopen/execve/posix_spawn/posix_spawnp) is deliberately ABSENT:
# the exe shape is the file-IO family only — children are armed by the
# driver's injection export, and an exported dlopen would collide with
# the runtime's own class-L definition at link.
